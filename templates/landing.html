YTMusicUltimate

A tweak for YouTube Music on iOS, with a companion server.

What it does

  • Synced lyrics with translations, right inside the YouTube Music app.
  • Song downloads that keep playing when you are offline.
  • Everything is served by this server, privately. See the privacy policy.

Dashboard

The dashboard is where the owner manages the server: lyrics library, logs, updates and device pairing.
